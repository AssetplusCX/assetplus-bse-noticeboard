Methodology. Notices are pulled directly from BSE's public "Notices & Circulars" board filtered to Segment = Mutual Fund (bseindia.com), which is exactly what the "StAR MF Notices" link on bsestarmf.in opens — no BSE StAR MF login is used or required. The board seeds from two seen-once snapshots (the notice archive's most recent page, 5–13 Mar 2026, and the current board as it stood on 21 Sep 2026) and then grows automatically: each scheduled capture re-reads BSE's live "current" listing and appends any notice number not already on this board, so nothing that appears there is lost even though BSE itself only ever shows its latest ~25. BSE's own archive extends further back than March 2026 via its own date-range search if deeper history is ever needed. "Topic" is a heuristic tag derived from each notice's subject line to make the board easier to scan; "Category" and "Department" are BSE's own fields, unedited.
